Backstory

Archibald "BlackDog" Whitefield was a student at the royal Percival Voth University of Arcane Studies. He was very skilled with herbs and alchemy, but always performed poorly at conventional magic. He is the firstborn of Lady Isolde of Marrowgate, keeper of the royal library, and the eldest child of the heir of House Whitefield, which takes its name from its cotton fields. He had an ordinary childhood, hunting regularly with his father and spending his days reading his mother's books. Unfortunately, his physical skills were poor: not particularly strong or fast, though healthy. That did not please his father, who wanted a hunter, a knight, a warrior. They used to call him the black sheep behind his back. His true talent was in books. He was always interested in botany, potions and biology. While hunting, he paid attention to the nature around him: poisonous plants, snakes, spiders, birds, scorpions and insects. He learned how to kill and clean animals and how a body works, and at home he read about biology, poisons, magic and alchemy.

When he came of age, he tried to win a seat in the royal university's classrooms, without success: his talent for magic was poor, to say the least. His parents arranged a place in the school of alchemy, which had less funding and fewer students, and that was perfect for him. Time passed, and he graduated in alchemy, specializing in herbs and medicinal alchemy. In his 22nd year he joined his first expedition. The realm did not pay for the journey, so he set out alone. On the road he met a Dahlan master, very good with herbs, from whom he learned a lot... even things he didn't want to...

Later that year, something happened that would change his life forever...

He discovered that his master was studying curses to use on the village's enemies. When confronted, the master tried to curse him to silence him, and a fight began. The curse was not completed and, convinced that everything was fine, Archibald returned home and continued his research. One day an accident happened in the lab: someone cut themselves. Without warning, Archibald turned into a monster with sharp teeth and claws, attacked everyone and destroyed the lab. After the incident, his former teacher, trying to save Archibald's life, let him go. But he could never return home, or he would be killed as a black magic user.

He joined the Brotherhood after this, hiding his name. After each transformation his body suffers severe pain and malnutrition, and his ankles hurt, so he needs a walking stick afterwards. Some missions with the Brotherhood went wrong, and thanks to the curse he survived them. Some began calling him "Black Cat", a bringer of bad luck, which reminded him of the time they called him "black sheep". But given how thin his body is, he decided "BlackDog" was a more fitting nickname.

Skills and Abilities

Abilities

The Curse

Triggered by the sight of blood. In his cursed form Archibald is fast and brutal, but he cannot tell friend from foe. Afterwards he is left weak, starving and in pain, and needs his walking stick to move.
